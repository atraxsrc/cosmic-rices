COSMIC Rices

14 themes, each with a dark and a light half. One rice recolours the whole desktop: COSMIC itself, terminals, shell, editors, Firefox, Brave, GTK and Qt apps, cursors and wallpapers.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/atraxsrc/themebox/master/install.sh | bash

Installs themebox. Then themebox get noir and themebox switch noir, using the name from any card, and flip dark and light from the panel.
